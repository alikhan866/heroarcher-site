Hero Archer Get the game

The forest is crawling.

Hero Archer is an idle tower defense game. The tower fights on its own and your archer never misses a shift. Your job is to make them stronger, run after run.

On an iPhone? Hero Archer is Android only for now. An iPhone version is in the works.

Your archer fights alone

Arrows fly at the nearest threat, and every kill pays coins.

Coins buy damage, fire rate, range and health for the current run, and they reset when it ends. The tutorial's advice holds up: damage first is never wrong.

  • Eight abilities on the bar

    Arrow Rain, Lightning Strike, Poison Cloud, Frost Snare, Mega Gust, Overdrive, Patch Up and Gold Rush. They run on energy, so you pick your moment.

  • One contraption per run

    The Storm Rod zaps the nearest enemy and arcs to the next. The Ballista skewers the toughest one in a line. The Barracks sends out a guard squad. Frost Bellows, the Steam Emitter and the Coin Mint are in there too.

  • Gear shows up on the archer

    Bows, hoods, cloaks, quivers, boots, rings and amulets, all with rolled stats. Bosses always drop something, and whatever you equip, your archer wears.

Waves and bosses

Every 5th wave brings a boss.

A skull on the wave bar warns you two waves out. Bosses wind up before the big hit, and a shove or a bolt at the right moment breaks it.

The chapters

Four lands, 50 waves each.

Clear the finale and the next land unlocks. Each one is tougher, and far richer.

Then the Nightmare lap. Clear all four and they come back meaner: one spawn in eight is an elite, and bosses arrive with a champion pack. The Weekly Trial changes the rules every Monday, with weeks like Swarm, where there are twice as many and they're half as tough.

The loop

Good hunting, hero.

Hero Archer is free on Google Play. The shards you earn go into Workshop upgrades that survive every defeat, so a bad run still counts.
