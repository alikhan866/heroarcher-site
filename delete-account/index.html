Account & Data Deletion

Hero Archer Tower Idle Defense

Developer: Freeze Gaming · Contact: khanxesthetix@gmail.com

Hero Archer Tower Idle Defense lets you optionally sign in with Google (Google Play Games) so your game progress is backed up to your own Google account. This page explains how to have that data deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email khanxesthetix@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Hero Archer data, sent from the Google account email you used to sign in.
  2. We delete the cloud save stored for your account within 30 days and confirm by reply. No other request steps are needed.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your cloud save (game progress backup) linked to your Google Play Games profile. Nothing is retained after deletion.
  • Kept: nothing. We run no accounts or servers of our own; the game keeps no server-side data about you beyond that cloud save.
  • Local data: progress stored on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.
  • Your Google Play Games profile itself belongs to your Google account and is managed by Google. You can also unlink the game or delete your Play Games profile data at myaccount.google.com/connections or in the Google Play Games app settings.
  • Advertising ID: handled by Google AdMob and resettable any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
