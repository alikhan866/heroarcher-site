Privacy Policy

Hero Archer Tower Idle Defense

Effective date: July 20, 2026
Developer: Freeze Gaming
Contact: support@heroarcher.com

Hero Archer Tower Idle Defense ("the game") is developed by Freeze Gaming. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

Data the game stores on your device only

  • Game progress (chapters, upgrades, currencies, settings, tutorial state) is saved locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Gameplay statistics used for game balancing are recorded locally.

Data collected by third-party services

The game uses the following services, which may collect data as described in their own policies:

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows optional, player-initiated rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions that require it, the game shows a consent form before any advertising data is processed. You can decline personalized ads; the game and its rewards work the same either way.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive purchase receipts (what was bought, when) to deliver your items, but never your payment details. See Google Play's terms and privacy policy.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account or registration is required or offered.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or microphone/camera data.
  • The game does not include third-party analytics SDKs beyond the advertising service described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service used by the game, contact us and we will help address it.

Your choices

  • Ads consent (EEA/UK): you can change your consent choice from the game's settings at any time.
  • Ad personalization: you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • Purchases: manage or refund purchases through Google Play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated effective date.

Contact
